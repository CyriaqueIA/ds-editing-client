Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data DS Editing collects on ds-editing.com, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

1. Who we are

DS Editing is the trade name of Silver Ledger HK LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming (USA) under number 2026-001894957, 1603 Capitol Ave, Suite 413G-2373, Cheyenne, WY 82001, USA. It is the data controller. Contact for anything about your data: contact@ds-editing.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Visit statistics (always, without cookies): the country you connect from (derived from your IP address, which we do not store in this case), the language you chose, the sections of the page you viewed, the time spent, the type of device and browser, and the site or link you came from. Purpose: understand which content is useful and which channels bring visitors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.
  • Detailed analytics and session recordings (only if you click “Accept”): your IP address, the buttons and links you click, and a recording of your navigation (mouse movements, scrolling, clicks) through PostHog. Everything you type in a field is masked and never recorded. A cookie lets us recognise you if you come back. Purpose: improve the site and our offer. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Contact form and private portfolio request: your name, email, studio or company, the type of project and your message. Purpose: reply to you and send what you asked for. Legal basis: steps taken at your request.
  • Gift offer in the private portfolio: your phone number and the name of your podcast. Purpose: call you to set up your free first edit. Legal basis: steps taken at your request.
  • Call booking: your name, email, phone number, studio, website, notes, time zone and chosen time slot. Purpose: organise the call, send the confirmation and a reminder. Legal basis: steps taken at your request.

When you fill in a form or book a call during a visit, we link that visit to your request so that we know which pages you saw before contacting us. We never sell your data and never use it for automated decision-making.

3. Cookies

Without your consent, we only use what is strictly necessary: remembering your language and your cookie choice (stored in your browser). With your consent, PostHog sets cookies to measure audience and record sessions. You can change your choice at any time:

Cookie settings

4. Who receives your data

Only the DS Editing team members who need it (management and sales). We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database: visits, requests, bookings), servers in Australia;
  • PostHog (analytics and session recordings), servers in the European Union (Germany);
  • FormSubmit (delivery of the contact form by email), USA;
  • Microsoft 365 (our email inbox) and Google (Google Meet video calls);
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting, technical logs), USA;
  • Telegram (internal notifications to our team when a visit, a request or a booking arrives).

Some of these providers are located outside the European Union. Transfers are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, depending on the provider.

5. How long we keep it

  • Visit statistics and session recordings: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Contact requests, portfolio requests and bookings: up to 3 years after our last exchange, unless you become a client (then for the duration of the business relationship and legal obligations).

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, ask for it to be restricted or receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to contact@ds-editing.com; we reply within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (for example the CNIL in France, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands, Datatilsynet in Denmark).

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
